ARCANE LITANIES Inscribed upon the armour and wargear of the Crimson King were formulae, wards and occult geometries intended to protect their bearer against the dangers inherent in drawing upon the Warp. Once per battle, when Magnus suffers a Wound as a result of Perils of the Warp, he may ignore that Wound. The Psychic Test and psychic power are otherwise resolved normally. PSYKER — MASTERY LEVEL 4 Magnus the Red is a Psyker with Mastery Level 4. Before the battle, Magnus selects five psychic powers in total. These powers may be selected from the following disciplines: Biomancy, Divination, Pyromancy, Telekinesis and Telepathy. Magnus must select powers from at least two different psychic disciplines. He may not select all five powers from a single discipline. Infernal Phoenix and Strands of Fate are always known by Magnus and count towards his five psychic powers. Magnus otherwise follows all normal ProHammer rules for Psykers unless specifically stated otherwise. Magnus counts as belonging to all five Prosperine Cults for the purposes of army selection and other friendly abilities, but gains no Cult Arcana or Cult Mastery from them. PSYCHIC SUPREMACY Magnus may never have more than two Blessings affecting himself at the same time. If Magnus successfully invokes a Blessing upon himself while already affected by two Blessings, he must immediately choose one existing Blessing affecting him to end before the new power takes effect. This restriction does not limit Blessings affecting friendly units or Maledictions affecting enemy units. Magnus may never have more than two psychic powers with ongoing effects simultaneously affecting himself and/or the opposing Primarch. LORD OF THE ETHER Magnus may invoke up to four psychic powers during each player turn, overriding the normal ProHammer maximum of two. Magnus may invoke multiple Witchfire powers during the same Shooting phase, subject to his normal limit of four psychic powers. Magnus may not successfully invoke the same psychic power more than once during the same player turn. Activating the Blade of Ahn-Nunurta as a Force Weapon does not count against this limit. THE CRIMSON KING The psychic mastery of Magnus was such that even a faltering connection to the Warp could be seized and brought under control through sheer force of will. Magnus may re-roll one failed Psychic Test during each player turn. The second result must be accepted. Magnus may attempt to Deny the Witch against any psychic power if either the Psyker invoking the power or its target is within 24" of Magnus. Resolve the Deny the Witch attempt normally using Magnus' own Mastery Level. Each psychic power may still only be the subject of one Deny the Witch attempt, as normal. THE WARP BENDS TO MAGNUS Where lesser psykers struggled as the immaterium became increasingly disturbed, Magnus imposed his will upon the Warp with a power few beings could hope to equal. Magnus ignores all Leadership penalties caused by Disturbance in the Warp. In addition, psychic powers successfully invoked by Magnus are ignored when determining the penalties caused by Disturbance in the Warp for other friendly Psykers. Psychic powers successfully invoked by other friendly Psykers still contribute towards Disturbance in the Warp normally. Magnus may also attempt to deny the witch at 18” around him. INFERNAL PHOENIX Witchfire — Beam Magnus unleashes a torrent of incandescent aetheric fire, burning through flesh and armour as it tears across the battlefield. Range 24" | Strength 8 | AP1 | Melta Resolve Infernal Phoenix using the normal rules for Beam psychic powers. STRANDS OF FATE Malediction Magnus reaches into the skeins of possibility and twists them about his victim, turning every intended action into another path towards failure. Choose one enemy non-Vehicle unit within 18" and line of sight. Until the beginning of Magnus’ next turn, that unit must pass a Leadership Test each time it attempts to: • Move during the Movement phase; • Shoot during the Shooting phase; or • declare a Charge. If the test is failed, the attempted action is lost and the unit may not attempt that type of action again during that phase. A separate Leadership Test is required for each different action attempted. SORCEROUS DUELLIST Even when locked in single combat with one of his brother Primarchs, Magnus remained first and foremost a master of the immaterium. While Magnus is fighting a Primarch Duel, at the beginning of each Assault phase he may invoke one of the following: • a Blessing which targets only Magnus; or • a Malediction which targets only the opposing Primarch. This psychic power counts towards Magnus’ maximum allowance of four powers for that player turn. All normal Psychic Tests, Deny the Witch attempts and Perils of the Warp rules apply. PRIMARCH RETINUE Magnus the Red may select any one of the following as his Primarch Retinue: • Legion Honour Guard Squad • Legion Terminator Command Squad • Sekhmet Terminator Cabal A retinue selected in this manner does not occupy an additional Force Organisation selection and otherwise follows the normal Primarch Retinue rules. THE CRIMSON KING’S GUARD A Legion Honour Guard Squad or Legion Terminator Command Squad selected as Magnus’ Primarch Retinue may be upgraded to a Brotherhood of Psykers (Mastery Level 1\) for +25 points per squad. A squad upgraded in this manner is assigned to one Prosperine Cult and receives the normal Cult Arcana and Cult Mastery benefits of that Cult. The squad selects one psychic power from any of the normal psychic disciplines available to Thousand Sons Psykers and otherwise follows all normal rules for a Psychic Brotherhood.
