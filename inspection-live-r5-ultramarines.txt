Legiones Astartes (Ultramarines): This Detachment is Ultramarines. Models with the Legiones Astartes special rule gain Legiones Astartes (Ultramarines). Legion-specific rules, units, wargear and characters are unlocked by this selection where applicable.
Ultramarines — Legion Rules & Armoury: LEGION SPECIAL RULES
CODEX ASTARTES
The XIII Legion possesses an extraordinarily flexible and disciplined command structure, allowing its forces to divide and reorganise rapidly as battlefield conditions demand.
A Legion Tactical Squad consisting of exactly ten models may be divided into two Combat Squads of five models each.
This decision is made before deployment.
The Sergeant, specialist weapons, heavy weapons, Legion Vexilla and Nuncio Vox may be divided between the two Combat Squads as the Ultramarines player wishes.
After being divided, each Combat Squad counts as a separate unit for deployment, Morale, casualties, scoring and all other purposes.
For Victory Points, each Combat Squad is worth half the original unit's total points value. Any remaining fraction is assigned to the squad containing the Sergeant.
If the original Tactical Squad purchased a Dedicated Transport, it must be assigned to one of the Combat Squads. Only that Combat Squad may begin the battle embarked within it.
FLEXIBLE COMMAND STRUCTURE
The Ultramarines maintain an unusually large and highly organised officer corps capable of directing complex operations without loss of cohesion.
An Ultramarines Detachment may include up to four HQ selections.
The normal limitation on models with Master of the Legion still applies.
In addition, an Ultramarines army begins the battle with one additional Strategy Point.
This Strategy Point follows all normal ProHammer rules.
ULTRAMARINES ARMOURY
The armouries of the XIII Legion reflect the ordered and pragmatic nature of the Ultramarines. Rather than favouring esoteric or excessively specialised weapons, the Legion prizes equipment of proven effectiveness, refined through centuries of warfare and manufactured to exacting standards throughout the Five Hundred Worlds.
LEGATINE AXE — 20 POINTS
Any Ultramarines Independent Character with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase a Legatine Axe for +20 points.
Weapon | Strength | Special Rules
Legatine Axe | User +1 | Power Weapon, Two-Handed
The Legatine Axe is a precisely balanced power weapon designed to combine the cutting force of an axe with the speed and control demanded by the warriors of Macragge.
MANTLE OF ULTRAMAR — 20 POINTS
An Ultramarines Praetor equipped with Artificer Armour may exchange it for a Mantle of Ultramar for +20 points.
A Mantle of Ultramar grants a 2+ Armour Save and Feel No Pain (5+). In addition, the bearer is immune to the effects of the Blind special rule.
This protection applies only to the bearer. A unit joined by the bearer may still be affected by Blind normally.
BREACHER POWER WEAPONS — +5 POINTS PER MODEL
Any model in an Ultramarines Legion Breacher Siege Squad may replace its Bolter with a Power Weapon for +5 points.
A model which replaces its Bolter in this manner may not also select another weapon which replaces its Bolter from the squad's Specialist Weapons options.
ULTRAMARINES RITE OF WAR:
THE LOGOS LECTORA
The Logos Lectora is the battlefield application of the immense body of tactical theory compiled by the XIII Legion. Through disciplined command, precise communication and carefully coordinated formations, an Ultramarines force may alter its method of war as the tactical situation demands.
EFFECTS
TACTICAL COMMAND
At the beginning of each Ultramarines player turn, choose one of the following three commands: Full March, Hold Fast or Retribution Strike.
The chosen command applies until the beginning of the next Ultramarines player turn. Only one command may be active at a time.
FULL MARCH
All Ultramarines non-Vehicle units may re-roll the D6 used to determine the additional distance of an Advance Move. The second result must be accepted.
HOLD FAST
Ultramarines non-Vehicle units which remain stationary during their Movement phase may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 when resolving First Fire or Overwatch Fire.
In addition, any Snap Fire attacks made by such a unit hit on a 5+ instead of the normal 6+. Ultramarines Dreadnoughts may also benefit from this command if they remained stationary.
RETRIBUTION STRIKE
All Ultramarines non-Vehicle units gain the Counter-Attack special rule.
An Ultramarines Dreadnought which was not already engaged and is successfully charged instead gains +1 Attack during that Assault phase.
LIMITATIONS
The Detachment must take one additional compulsory HQ choice. This additional choice must be a Legion Master of Signals Consul or a Damocles Command Rhino.
The Detachment must take one additional compulsory Troops choice.
The Detachment may not include more Vehicles with the Tank or Flyer type than it includes Infantry units.
Units in the Detachment may not deploy using Infiltrate or enter play using Deep Strike. Normal Reserves may still be used.
Units which are required to enter play using Deep Strike, such as Legion Drop Pods, may not be selected.
ULTRAMARINES RITE OF WAR:
VIGIL OPERTII MISSION
The Vigil Opertii are one of the less celebrated instruments of Ultramar's rule, employing veteran Legionaries and carefully selected mortal auxiliaries in counter-insurgency, reconnaissance and internal security operations throughout the Five Hundred Worlds.
EFFECTS
VIGIL AUXILIA
All Infantry units in the army's allied Imperialis Militia Detachment gain the Infiltrate special rule.
SACRED DUTY
All Infantry units in the allied Imperialis Militia Detachment gain Implacable Advance and count as Scoring Units regardless of their normal battlefield role.
OVERSEERS
Ultramarines Legion Reconnaissance Squads lose the Support Squad special rule and may therefore be selected to fulfil compulsory Troops choices.
LIMITATIONS
This Rite of War may only be used by a Loyalist Ultramarines Detachment.
The army must include an Allied Detachment drawn from the Imperialis Militia & Cults Army List.
The Allied Detachment must possess both the Gene-crafted and Warrior Elite Provenances of War.
The Allied Detachment may not include Inducted Levy Squads.
The Ultramarines Detachment must include a Legion Vigilator Consul.

RULE Source Entry: Primarch of the Ultramarines, Lord of Ultramar, the Avenging Son
Roboute Guilliman was among the greatest commanders and statesmen of the Great Crusade, a Primarch whose genius lay not merely in winning battles, but in understanding the greater structure of war itself. Under his rule the worlds of Ultramar became a realm of extraordinary order and prosperity, while the XIII Legion grew into one of the largest and most disciplined forces of the Legiones Astartes.
Upon the battlefield Guilliman sought neither reckless glory nor rigid adherence to a single doctrine. Every engagement was studied, every success and failure considered, and theory was continually tested against the practical realities of war. His warriors were expected to adapt as swiftly as their Primarch, changing formation and method as circumstances demanded until the enemy was confronted with precisely the form of warfare it was least prepared to resist.

Weapon Range S AP Type Arbitrator 18" 6 3 Assault 2, Rending, Master-crafted MASTER STRATEGIST Few commanders possessed Guilliman’s ability to comprehend the flow of an entire battlefield and translate that understanding into precise orders. PRETERNATURAL STRATEGY Guilliman’s ability to reassess a battle plan in moments allowed the XIII Legion to alter its dispositions even as the enemy committed itself to battle. After both armies have deployed but before the first turn begins, nominate one friendly Ultramarines unit. That unit may be redeployed anywhere it could legally have been deployed at the beginning of the battle. It must obey all normal deployment restrictions and may not be placed into Reserve unless it was already eligible to begin the battle in Reserve. SIRE OF ULTRAMAR The warriors of the XIII Legion fought beneath the gaze of a Primarch who demanded discipline in victory and adversity alike. Friendly Ultramarines units with at least one model within 12" of Roboute Guilliman may re-roll failed Morale tests. The second result must be accepted. THEORETICAL / PRACTICAL To Guilliman, no doctrine was valuable merely because it had succeeded before. Theory existed to be tested in battle, and practical experience existed to refine theory. At the beginning of each Ultramarines turn, choose one of the following doctrines. The chosen doctrine affects friendly Ultramarines units with at least one model within 18" of Roboute Guilliman until the beginning of the next Ultramarines turn. ADVANCE Affected units may move up to 1" further than normal during their Movement phase. FIRE Affected units may re-roll shooting To Hit rolls of 1. ASSAULT Affected units may re-roll close combat To Hit rolls of 1.
