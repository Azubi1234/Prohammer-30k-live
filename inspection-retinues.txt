  RULE Sons Of Horus — Legion Rules & Armoury: LEGION SPECIAL RULES
CLOSE RANGE BRUTALITY
The Sons of Horus favour overwhelming close-range assaults in which gunfire and blade work become almost indistinguishable.
At the beginning of the Assault phase, a Sons of Horus unit which is otherwise able to charge may fire at the enemy unit it intends to charge.
Models armed with Rapid Fire, Pistol or Assault weapons may fire.
Template weapons may not be used.
These attacks hit only on a 5+, regardless of Ballistic Skill, and never cause Morale or Pinning tests.
Firing in this manner does not prevent the unit from charging, but it must charge the unit it fired upon.
If casualties leave the target outside charge range, or destroy it completely, the Sons of Horus unit may not declare a charge against another enemy unit.
Instead, it moves D6" directly towards the target's current position, or its final position if it was destroyed.
This movement is not a charge and may not bring the unit within 1" of another enemy model.
TIP OF THE SPEAR
The Sons of Horus excel at decapitation strikes and sudden assaults upon the most important point in the enemy line.
Even if the mission does not normally permit Deep Strike, the following may deploy using Deep Strike:
• one Sons of Horus Infantry unit selected from Elites;
• one Sons of Horus Independent Character, either alone or joined by a Command Squad.
These units must begin the battle in Reserve.
PRIDE OF THE WARMASTER
The Sons of Horus possess an arrogance born of countless victories and the favour of the Warmaster himself
At the end of the final scheduled game turn, the opposing player may demand that one additional complete game turn be played.
Roll a D6. On a 4+, one additional complete game turn is played. This roll may only be made once per battle.
This rule does not extend a mission which has already ended because a specific objective or action was completed.
SONS OF HORUS ARMOURY
As the favoured Legion of the Warmaster, the Sons of Horus enjoyed access to enormous reserves of arms and munitions. Alongside these Imperial weapons survived the brutal traditions of Cthonia, where weapons intended for gang warfare and murder remained prized even amongst the Legion's highest officers.
BANESTRIKE AMMUNITION — 5 POINTS PER MODEL
Sons of Horus Seeker Squads, Justaerin Terminator Squads and Independent Characters equipped with a Bolter, Foeblaster Boltgun, Combi-Bolter, Storm Bolter or Combi-Weapon may purchase Banestrike Ammunition for +5 points per eligible model.
Every eligible model in a squad must purchase the upgrade, or none may do so. Models which have replaced their bolt weapon with another weapon neither pay for nor benefit from Banestrike Ammunition.
When firing Banestrike Ammunition, use the following profile where appropriate:
Weapon | Range | Strength | AP | Special Rules
Banestrike Bolter | 18" | 4 | 4 | Rapid Fire, Banestrike
BANESTRIKE
A natural To Wound roll of 6 made with Banestrike Ammunition is resolved at AP3.
When Banestrike Ammunition is used with a Foeblaster Boltgun, Storm Bolter, Combi-Bolter or the bolter component of a Combi-Weapon, retain the weapon's normal number of shots and firing type, but reduce its range to 18", use AP4 and apply the Banestrike special rule.
Banestrike Ammunition may not be combined with Special Issue Ammunition or another ammunition upgrade.
CTHONIAN CULLING BLADE — 10 POINTS
Any Sons of Horus Independent Character or squad Sergeant with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase a Cthonian Culling Blade for +10 points.
Weapon | Strength | Special Rules
Cthonian Culling Blade | User | Rending (5+)
RENDING (5+)
A Cthonian Culling Blade follows the normal ProHammer rules for Rending, except that the effect is triggered on a natural To Wound roll of 5 or 6.
Against Vehicles, Rending is still triggered only by a natural Armour Penetration roll of 6.
A Cthonian Culling Blade does not count as a Power Weapon.
CHAINAXE — 4 POINTS
Any Sons of Horus Character eligible to select a Close Combat Weapon may instead select a Chainaxe for +4 points.
Weapon | Strength | Special Rules
Chainaxe | User | Chainaxe
A Chainaxe follows the normal rules presented in the Legiones Astartes Army List.
TELEPORTATION TRANSPONDERS
Any Sons of Horus unit composed entirely of models wearing any form of Terminator Armour may purchase Teleportation Transponders for +15 points per unit. A Sons of Horus Independent Character wearing any form of Terminator Armour may purchase them for +10 points.
A model or unit equipped with Teleportation Transponders gains the Deep Strike special rule and may deploy using Deep Strike even if the mission would not normally permit it.
An Independent Character intending to Deep Strike as part of another unit must purchase Teleportation Transponders separately.
SONS OF HORUS RITE OF WAR:
THE LONG MARCH
After Isstvan V, the Sons of Horus formed the spearhead of the Warmaster's advance upon Terra, an all-conquering host that drove relentlessly through the Imperium and swept aside world after world in its path.
EFFECTS
RELENTLESS MARCH
At the beginning of each Sons of Horus player turn, determine the position of the majority of the surviving models in each Sons of Horus Infantry unit. The unit gains the appropriate special rule until the beginning of the next Sons of Horus player turn:
• Within the Sons of Horus deployment zone — Relentless.
• Outside either player's deployment zone — Fleet.
• Within the enemy deployment zone — Crusader.
If a unit is equally divided between two areas, the controlling player chooses which applicable rule it gains.
THE WARMASTER'S PORTION
During the first Sons of Horus player turn, all units in the Detachment may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1.
In addition, Legion Terminator Squads may be selected as non-compulsory Troops choices.
They may not fulfil compulsory Troops selections.
LIMITATIONS
This Rite of War may only be selected by a Traitor Sons of Horus Detachment.
The Detachment may not include models with the Slow and Purposeful special rule unless they begin the battle embarked aboard a Transport or enter play using Deep Strike.
The Detachment may not include a Fortification or an Allied Detachment.
SONS OF HORUS RITE OF WAR:
THE BLACK REAVING
The Black Reaving embodies the Cthonian heritage of the XVI Legion. Rather than engaging the enemy in a single ordered battle line, Reaver companies and supporting formations encircle their prey, isolate individual formations and fall upon them from several directions like the gangs of ancient Cthonia.
EFFECTS
REAVER ONSLAUGHT
Reaver Attack Squads may be selected as Troops choices and may fulfil compulsory Troops selections.
At least one of the Detachment's compulsory Troops choices must be a Reaver Attack Squad.
CTHONIAN ENCIRCLEMENT
After both armies have been selected, nominate up to two Sons of Horus Infantry units. These units gain the Outflank special rule.
Any Dedicated Transport purchased for one of these units also gains Outflank and enters play together with its passengers.
An Independent Character which begins the battle attached to one of these nominated units may Outflank with it.
When one of the two nominated units enters play, the Sons of Horus player may choose the side table edge from which that unit enters instead of rolling to determine the edge. The other nominated unit follows the normal ProHammer Outflank rules.
LODGE CEREMONY
Once per battle, at the beginning of a Sons of Horus player turn, the Warlord may declare a Lodge Ceremony.
Until the end of that player turn, all non-Vehicle Sons of Horus units gain the Fleet special rule.
CUT THEM APART
During the first round of a close combat, a Sons of Horus unit which charged an enemy unit that was already engaged with another friendly Sons of Horus unit may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1.
This benefit applies only during the player turn in which the unit charged.
LIMITATIONS
The Detachment must include a Legion Centurion upgraded to a Master of Signals Consul.
At least one compulsory Troops choice must be a Reaver Attack Squad.
The Detachment must include at least as many Fast Attack choices as Heavy Support choices.
The Detachment may not include a Fortification or an Allied Detachment.
  RULE Word Bearers — Legion Rules & Armoury: LEGION SPECIAL RULES
FANATICAL DEVOTION
The Word Bearers are driven by a religious fervour which allows them to endure hardship and terror through sheer certainty of purpose.
Non-Daemon units with the Legiones Astartes (Word Bearers) special rule may re-roll failed Morale and Pinning tests.
THE DARK SHEPHERDS
The Word Bearers do not march to war without spiritual leaders to guide their warriors.
A Word Bearers Detachment must include at least one Legion Chaplain Consul or Legion-specific Dark Apostle as an HQ selection.
A character specifically stated to count as a Dark Apostle also fulfils this requirement.
RITUAL OF CONSECRATION
Traitor Word Bearers regard every battlefield as sacred ground upon which blood may be offered to the powers they serve.
When a non-Daemon Word Bearers unit completely destroys an enemy unit during the Assault Phase, it may forgo its Consolidation move.
If it does so, nominate one friendly Daemonic Covenant unit currently in Reserve. That unit may re-roll its next Reserve roll.
Only one Covenant unit may receive this benefit each turn.
This rule has no effect in a Loyalist Word Bearers army.
DAEMONIC COVENANT
The Word Bearers were the first Legion to willingly bind the entities of the Warp to their cause. Through sacrifice and profane ritual, they learned to tear openings in reality through which these creatures could be summoned to war. A Traitor Word Bearers army may include a Daemons of the Ruinstorm Covenant Detachment, selected from the Daemons of the Ruinstorm Army List.
DAEMONS OF THE RUINSTORM COVENANT DETACHMENT
Selection | Allowance
HQ | 0–1
Troops | 1–3
Elites | 0–1
Fast Attack | 0–1
Heavy Support | 0–1
An HQ choice is not compulsory, but at least one Troops choice must be selected.
The Covenant Detachment may cost no more than 25% of the army's total points limit and has no minimum points requirement.
Units are purchased using their normal profiles, options, Dominions and special rules from the Daemons of the Ruinstorm Army List.
The Covenant Detachment counts as the army's Allied Detachment and does not require additional permission to use. A Word Bearers army may not include another Allied Detachment unless a rule specifically states otherwise. Covenant units may not fulfil compulsory selections in the primary Word Bearers Detachment and may not provide the army's Warlord. Word Bearers and Covenant units are treated as Fellow Warriors. Independent Characters from one Detachment may not join units from the other.
DAEMONIC SUMMONING
All units in a Daemonic Covenant Detachment must begin the battle in Reserve.
They may not deploy normally, Infiltrate or Outflank, but instead enter play using the following rules.
When a Covenant unit becomes available from Reserve, nominate one friendly Summoning Point already on the battlefield.
The following count as Summoning Points:
Personal Icons, Icons of Chaos Undivided, Accursed Crozii, and any other rule specifically stated to act as a Summoning Point.
Place the centre model of the summoned unit within 6" of the Summoning Point and more than 2" from any enemy model. The unit then enters play using the normal Deep Strike rules.
If no eligible Summoning Point or legal position is available, the unit remains in Reserve and must roll for Reserves again during a later turn.
A Summoning Point may be used while its bearer is engaged in close combat and is not consumed when used. The same Summoning Point may summon multiple units during the battle.
Any Deep Strike Mishap is resolved normally.
Units summoned in this manner follow all normal restrictions for units arriving by Deep Strike.
DAEMONOLOGY
Daemonic Covenant is separate from Daemonology (Malefic). Units created through Conjuration psychic powers are not Covenant units and do not use the Daemonic Covenant rules.
THE DARK BRETHREN
FROM BEYOND
A Word Bearers army using The Dark Brethren may form a far deeper pact with the entities of the Warp.
If the army includes a Daemons of the Ruinstorm Covenant Detachment, the following changes apply:
The Covenant may use the normal Daemons of the Ruinstorm Allied Detachment Force Organisation Chart instead of the restricted Covenant chart. The normal 25% points limit is removed. The Detachment instead follows the normal points restrictions for Allied Detachments.
Word Bearers and the Daemons of the Ruinstorm are treated as Sworn Brothers.
Before deployment, each Daemon unit must be designated as either Manifested or Summoned.
Manifested units deploy and enter play normally using the rules from the Daemons of the Ruinstorm Army List.
Summoned units must begin the battle in Reserve and enter play using the Daemonic Summoning rules above.
A Word Bearers army may still include only one Daemons of the Ruinstorm Allied Detachment.
WORD BEARERS ARMOURY
The armouries of the XVII Legion contain an ever-growing collection of occult weapons, blasphemous relics and artefacts touched by the Warp. Many of these objects would once have been destroyed by the Imperial Heralds themselves, but now serve as symbols of the Primordial Truth.
ACCURSED CROZIUS — 40 POINTS
Any Word Bearers Praetor or Centurion may purchase an Accursed Crozius for +40 points. A model equipped with an Accursed Crozius counts as a Dark Apostle for the purposes of The Dark Shepherds.
Weapon | Strength | Special Rules
Accursed Crozius | User | Power Weapon
The bearer receives a 4+ Invulnerable Save and counts as possessing a Personal Icon for the purposes of summoning Daemons.
A model may never possess more than one Accursed Crozius.
TAINTED WEAPON — SAME COST AS A POWER WEAPON
Any Word Bearers Character eligible to select a Power Weapon may instead select a Tainted Weapon for the same points cost.
Weapon | Strength | Special Rules
Tainted Weapon | User | Specialist Weapon, Tainted Strike
TAINTED STRIKE
Any unsaved wound inflicted by a Tainted Weapon becomes a Massive Wound and inflicts D3 Wounds instead of 1.
A Tainted Weapon is not a Power Weapon and does not ignore Armour Saves.
BURNING LORE — 30 POINTS
Any Word Bearers Praetor, Centurion, Chaplain or Diabolist which is not already a Psyker may purchase Burning Lore for +30 points.
The model becomes a Psyker with Mastery Level 1 and selects one psychic power from either the Biomancy or Telepathy discipline.
The model follows all normal ProHammer rules for Psykers.
HEX-BOLTS — 5 POINTS PER UNIT
Any Word Bearers Infantry unit equipped with Bolt weapons may purchase Hex-Bolts for +5 points per unit.
Bolt Pistols, Bolters, Combi-Bolters, Storm Bolters and the Bolter component of Combi-Weapons carried by models in the unit gain the Soul Blaze special rule.
Hex-Bolts may not be combined with Special Issue Ammunition or another ammunition upgrade.
ICON OF CHAOS UNDIVIDED — 30 POINTS
One model in a Legion Command Squad, Legion Veteran Squad or Legion Terminator Squad may carry an Icon of Chaos Undivided for +30 points. The Icon counts as a summoning point for the purposes of the Word Bearers' Daemonic Covenant.
In addition, friendly non-Daemon Word Bearers units with at least one model within 6" of the bearer gain the Fearless special rule.
FAVOUR OF THE PANTHEON
One non-named Word Bearers Independent Character in the army may purchase one of the following Favours of the Pantheon. A model may never possess more than one Favour.
Daemonic Aura — 15 points: The model gains a 5+ Invulnerable Save.
Daemonic Mutation — 15 points: The model gains +1 Attack.
Daemonic Strength — 10 points: The model gains +1 Strength.
Daemonic Wings — 20 points: The model becomes Jump Infantry. This may not be combined with a Jump Pack, Bike, Jetbike or Terminator Armour.
Daemonic Visage — 5 points: An enemy unit which loses a close combat involving the bearer suffers an additional -1 Leadership when taking the resulting Morale test.
The effects of a Favour are already included when determining the model's characteristics or special rules and may not be purchased more than once.
WORD BEARERS CONSUL:
DIABOLIST — +35 POINTS
A Word Bearers Centurion may be upgraded to a Diabolist for +35 points.
The Diabolist gains the Daemon and Preferred Enemy (Loyalists) special rules.
A Diabolist may not select a Bike, Jetbike, any form of Terminator Armour, Power Fist or Thunder Hammer.
The presence of at least one Diabolist allows eligible units in the Detachment to purchase Dark Channelling.
A Diabolist remains a Centurion for the purposes of purchasing an Accursed Crozius. A Diabolist equipped with an Accursed Crozius therefore counts as a Dark Apostle for the purposes of The Dark Shepherds.
DARK CHANNELLING — 25 POINTS PER UNIT
If the Detachment includes at least one Diabolist, a Legion Tactical Squad, Legion Veteran Squad, Legion Breacher Siege Squad, Legion Terminator Squad or Legion Assault Squad may purchase Dark Channelling for +25 points per squad.
After both armies have deployed but before the first turn begins, roll a D6 separately for each unit with Dark Channelling:
1–3 — Zealot: The unit gains the Zealot special rule for the duration of the battle.
4–5 — Unholy Strength: Models in the unit gain +1 Strength for the duration of the battle.
6 — Daemon: The unit gains the Daemon special rule for the duration of the battle. The unit may no longer count as a Scoring Unit. In a mission using Victory Points, the unit also counts as destroyed at the end of the battle even if it survives.
The effects of Dark Channelling apply only to the models which form part of the upgraded squad. Independent Characters or other models which subsequently join the unit do not gain its effects.
WORD BEARERS RITE OF WAR:
THE DARK BRETHREN
Before the corruption of the XVII Legion became manifest, the Word Bearers were renowned for their cohesion and self-sacrifice. After their fall, these same qualities were turned towards darker purposes, entire warbands offering blood and souls to the powers beyond reality in exchange for their favour.
EFFECTS
ARCH-TRAITORS
All Word Bearers Independent Characters in the Detachment gain Preferred Enemy against models belonging to the Loyalist faction.
SIGNS AND PORTENTS
After deployment but before the first turn begins, select one Word Bearers Troops unit and roll a D6.
On a 1–3, all enemy units gain Preferred Enemy against the selected unit for the duration of the battle.
On a 4–6, the selected Word Bearers unit gains Preferred Enemy against all enemy units for the duration of the battle.
FROM BEYOND
A Word Bearers Detachment using this Rite may include an Allied Detachment selected from Codex: Chaos Daemons. This Allied Detachment is treated as Sworn Brothers with the Word Bearers.
This does not prevent the Word Bearers from using their normal Daemonic Covenant rules.
HELL FOLLOWS WITH THEM
Whenever an enemy Psyker suffers a Wound from Perils of the Warp, that Wound becomes a Massive Wound and inflicts D3 Wounds instead of 1.
LIMITATIONS
This Rite may only be selected by a Traitor Word Bearers Detachment.
The Detachment must include at least one Diabolist.
The Detachment may include no more than one Heavy Support choice.
The army may not include a Fortification or an Allied Detachment drawn from another Space Marine Legion.
Any Allied Detachment other than Chaos Daemons is treated as Desperate Allies.
WORD BEARERS RITE OF WAR:
LAST OF THE SERRATED SUN
The Serrated Sun were the first of Lorgar's sons to willingly walk the Path of the Primordial Truth. Their survivors retained the Chapter's mastery of orbital assault warfare, descending upon their enemies alongside the monstrous Gal Vorbak in sudden and overwhelming attacks.
EFFECTS
COMPANY OF MONSTERS
Gal Vorbak Dark Brethren may be selected as Troops choices and may fulfil compulsory Troops selections.
Every Gal Vorbak unit in the Detachment must purchase either a Legion Drop Pod or Dreadclaw Drop Pod as a Dedicated Transport.
DROP ELITE
Any Word Bearers Infantry unit which may normally purchase a Rhino as a Dedicated Transport may instead purchase a Legion Drop Pod at its normal points cost.
BURNING SUN
Whenever a Legion Drop Pod or Dreadclaw Drop Pod belonging to this Detachment arrives using Deep Strike, every enemy unit with at least one model within 12" of the vehicle's final position must immediately take a Pinning test.
A unit need only take one Pinning test for each arriving Drop Pod, regardless of how many of its models are within 12".
LIMITATIONS
This Rite may only be selected by a Traitor Word Bearers Detachment.
All Infantry units in the Detachment must begin the battle in Reserve and enter play using a Deep Striking Drop Pod, Teleportation Transponders or embarked aboard a Transport with the Flyer type.
The army may not include Immobile units.
The army may not include a Fortification or an Allied Detachment.
  RULE Primarch's Chosen: Requirements: include the Primarch of the Legion; the Primarch is Warlord; army at least 1,500 points. Effects: the Primarch fulfils the compulsory HQ requirement and may be fielded from 1,500 points; Legion Veteran and Terminator Squads may be Troops and must provide the two compulsory Troops; the Primarch may take an Honour Guard or appropriate Legion bodyguard as a slotless retinue. Limitations: no other Lord of War; no Allied Detachment; no more than one other Master of the Legion; at least half the army's non-vehicle units must have Legiones Astartes; if the Primarch is destroyed all other units in the Detachment cease to be Scoring for the rest of the battle, but may contest.

Where lesser psykers risked being overwhelmed by the turbulence of the Warp, the presence of the Crimson King seemed instead to force that turbulence into obedience.

HORNED RAIMENT The war-plate of the Crimson King was as much an artefact of sorcery as of the artificer’s craft, surrounded by kine shields, illusions and shifting aetheric wards which distorted the form of Magnus and those who fought at his side. The Horned Raiment counts as Primarch Armour. When an enemy unit makes shooting attacks against Magnus or a unit he has joined, all To Hit rolls made as part of that attack suffer a -1 modifier. To Hit rolls made with Blast weapons suffer a -2 modifier instead. These modifiers have no effect in close combat. BLADE OF AHN-NUNURTA A weapon of ancient and esoteric provenance, the Blade of Ahn-Nunurta served as both a weapon of war and a focus through which Magnus could channel his immense psychic power. The Blade of Ahn-Nunurta is a Master-crafted, Two-Handed Force Weapon. It follows all normal ProHammer rules for Force Weapons. ARCANE LITANIES Inscribed upon the armour and wargear of the Crimson King were formulae, wards and occult geometries intended to protect their bearer against the dangers inherent in drawing upon the Warp. Once per battle, when Magnus suffers a Wound as a result of Perils of the Warp, he may ignore that Wound. The Psychic Test and psychic power are otherwise resolved normally. PSYKER — MASTERY LEVEL 4 Magnus the Red is a Psyker with Mastery Level 4. Before the battle, Magnus selects five psychic powers in total. These powers may be selected from the following disciplines: Biomancy, Divination, Pyromancy, Telekinesis and Telepathy. Magnus must select powers from at least two different psychic disciplines. He may not select all five powers from a single discipline. Infernal Phoenix and Strands of Fate are always known by Magnus and count towards his five psychic powers. Magnus otherwise follows all normal ProHammer rules for Psykers unless specifically stated otherwise. Magnus counts as belonging to all five Prosperine Cults for the purposes of army selection and other friendly abilities, but gains no Cult Arcana or Cult Mastery from them. PSYCHIC SUPREMACY Magnus may never have more than two Blessings affecting himself at the same time. If Magnus successfully invokes a Blessing upon himself while already affected by two Blessings, he must immediately choose one existing Blessing affecting him to end before the new power takes effect. This restriction does not limit Blessings affecting friendly units or Maledictions affecting enemy units. Magnus may never have more than two psychic powers with ongoing effects simultaneously affecting himself and/or the opposing Primarch. LORD OF THE ETHER Magnus may invoke up to four psychic powers during each player turn, overriding the normal ProHammer maximum of two. Magnus may invoke multiple Witchfire powers during the same Shooting phase, subject to his normal limit of four psychic powers. Magnus may not successfully invoke the same psychic power more than once during the same player turn. Activating the Blade of Ahn-Nunurta as a Force Weapon does not count against this limit. THE CRIMSON KING The psychic mastery of Magnus was such that even a faltering connection to the Warp could be seized and brought under control through sheer force of will. Magnus may re-roll one failed Psychic Test during each player turn. The second result must be accepted. Magnus may attempt to Deny the Witch against any psychic power if either the Psyker invoking the power or its target is within 24" of Magnus. Resolve the Deny the Witch attempt normally using Magnus' own Mastery Level. Each psychic power may still only be the subject of one Deny the Witch attempt, as normal. THE WARP BENDS TO MAGNUS Where lesser psykers struggled as the immaterium became increasingly disturbed, Magnus imposed his will upon the Warp with a power few beings could hope to equal. Magnus ignores all Leadership penalties caused by Disturbance in the Warp. In addition, psychic powers successfully invoked by Magnus are ignored when determining the penalties caused by Disturbance in the Warp for other friendly Psykers. Psychic powers successfully invoked by other friendly Psykers still contribute towards Disturbance in the Warp normally. Magnus may also attempt to deny the witch at 18” around him. INFERNAL PHOENIX Witchfire — Beam Magnus unleashes a torrent of incandescent aetheric fire, burning through flesh and armour as it tears across the battlefield. Range 24" | Strength 8 | AP1 | Melta Resolve Infernal Phoenix using the normal rules for Beam psychic powers. STRANDS OF FATE Malediction Magnus reaches into the skeins of possibility and twists them about his victim, turning every intended action into another path towards failure. Choose one enemy non-Vehicle unit within 18" and line of sight. Until the beginning of Magnus’ next turn, that unit must pass a Leadership Test each time it attempts to: • Move during the Movement phase; • Shoot during the Shooting phase; or • declare a Charge. If the test is failed, the attempted action is lost and the unit may not attempt that type of action again during that phase. A separate Leadership Test is required for each different action attempted. SORCEROUS DUELLIST Even when locked in single combat with one of his brother Primarchs, Magnus remained first and foremost a master of the immaterium. While Magnus is fighting a Primarch Duel, at the beginning of each Assault phase he may invoke one of the following: • a Blessing which targets only Magnus; or • a Malediction which targets only the opposing Primarch. This psychic power counts towards Magnus’ maximum allowance of four powers for that player turn. All normal Psychic Tests, Deny the Witch attempts and Perils of the Warp rules apply. PRIMARCH RETINUE Magnus the Red may select any one of the following as his Primarch Retinue: • Legion Honour Guard Squad • Legion Terminator Command Squad • Sekhmet Terminator Cabal A retinue selected in this manner does not occupy an additional Force Organisation selection and otherwise follows the normal Primarch Retinue rules. A squad upgraded in this manner is assigned to one Prosperine Cult and receives the normal Cult Arcana and Cult Mastery benefits of that Cult. The squad selects one psychic power from any of the normal psychic disciplines available to Thousand Sons Psykers and otherwise follows all normal rules for a Psychic Brotherhood.
